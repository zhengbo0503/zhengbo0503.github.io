Zhengbo Zhou

Research Associate · University of Leicester

Email · CV · GitHub · Google Scholar · ORCID

My research focuses on numerical linear algebra, matrix analysis, and high-performance computing. I develop mixed-precision Jacobi algorithms for accurately computing eigenvalues and eigenvectors of symmetric positive definite matrices, and singular values and singular vectors of general matrices.

I work with Dr. Behnam Hashemi at Leicester. See my publications for papers and accompanying research code. My blog contains research announcements and notes on numerical linear algebra and MATLAB.
