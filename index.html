About Me

Email · CV · GitHub · Google Scholar · ORCID

I am a Research Associate at the University of Leicester, working with Dr. Behnam Hashemi. I am currently finishing my Ph.D. in the Numerical Linear Algebra Group, Department of Mathematics, University of Manchester, supervised by Dr. Marcus Webb and Prof. Françoise Tisseur.

My research interest lies on numerical linear algebra, matrix analysis and high performance computing. My particular focus is on developing mixed-precision Jacobi algorithms for computing accurate eigensystems of symmetric positive definite matrix and singular value system for general matrix.
